Anthropic opens at $111.50 after the biggest IPO on record

Anthropic priced its shares at $83 on Wednesday evening, above the $70–78 range it marketed, and upsized the deal to about 980 million shares, raising $81.3 billion. That beats the SpaceX record set in June.

Trading in ANT didn't start until just before noon Eastern, and the first print came 34% above the offer price, putting the company near $2 trillion. Shares cooled to about $100 within minutes, so expect a volatile first day.
